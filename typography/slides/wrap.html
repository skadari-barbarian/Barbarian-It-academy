Syntax

overflow-wrap: break-word;

text-indent: 30px;


Explanation

  • overflow-wrap: normal; → Long words may overflow outside the container.
  • overflow-wrap: break-word; → Breaks long words only when necessary.
  • overflow-wrap: anywhere; → Allows breaking at any point to prevent overflow.
  • text-indent: 0; → No indentation.
  • text-indent: 30px; → Indents the first line by 30 pixels.
  • text-indent: 60px; → Indents the first line by 60 pixels.
